How your privacy works here

This tool has no server and no login. Every company you add is saved directly in this browser's local storage, on this device only. Nothing is ever transmitted anywhere — not to the person who built this page, not to any company, not to anyone. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, your data won't follow unless you export and re-import it below.

What this page is for

This is only for backing up or restoring your whole pipeline as a single file. It is not for uploading a resume or a JD — that upload lives inside the "Add / Edit company" form instead, in the "Upload JD / notes document" field.
